You have a problem.

"I want make an exact replica of this 2D character out of polymer clay and put them in a jar for me to stare at, but I can't! What do I do instead to fill this void?!"

If that sounds like something you would say, even a little bit, then I would encourage you to seek amateur help.

Luckily for you, that's me. And I might have the solution for you, dear reader. It sounds like you need a wallpaper for your electronic device.

You could just download someone else's much cooler picture and use that, but what if that's not something you want to do?

This website is for the 0.000001% of people who are like me. People who had a random thought one day and decided it would be a good idea to spend two hours drawing over a screenshot to make sure their favourite fictional characters fit with the specific colour theming they use on their device.

So... tracing. 100%. Should only be for personal, non-commercial use. (Like wallpapers.) Otherwise it feels really weird. Among other reasons of course. Just be honest about it.

As long as you have opaque pens, layer folders, and Alpha Lock, this should be pretty easy. There's nothing fancy. If your pen strokes are shaky, stablisation will probably be needed.

If you have those things equipped, then you're ready for the next part.

Or you can check the gallery and see what wallpapers I've made with this.
